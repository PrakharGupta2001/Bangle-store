# Asset provenance and licences

All 24 SVG images under `apps/storefront/public/images/` are original geometric illustrations authored for this Phase 1 demo. Their source generator is `scripts/init-demo.mjs`. No product photographs, third-party artwork, external image service or company asset is used. Illustrations do not establish the actual appearance, materials or stock of a product.

The project author permits use, modification and redistribution of these SVG illustrations and their generator with this Daya Variety Store project without an attribution requirement. There is no third-party photo licence to transfer.

The brand mark and decorative shapes are original text/CSS treatments. Fonts are the device's system fonts (Georgia/Times, Arial/Helvetica and installed Indic fallbacks); no font files are copied, redistributed or downloaded by the build. Rendering may vary between devices.

Public dependencies retain their own licences; consult the respective installed package LICENSE files. Dependencies are not bundled in the source ZIP. Exact packages are recorded in package-lock.json. The ZIP contains source and demo assets only.
